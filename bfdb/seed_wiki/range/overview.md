# The Training Range

The training range is a separate Vector Strike server for practice. There is
no campaign on it: no lives, no points, no sides to pick and no slot rules.
Every slot is open and almost everything you fly is graded, from a single bomb
to a carrier pass, a tanker join or a missile you defended against. The
results go to the range site with a full debrief.

It runs on the **Mariana Islands** map, laid out on the real Mariana Islands
Range Complex around Guam, Rota, Tinian, Saipan and Farallon de Medinilla.

## Joining

Look for **`[VS] Vector Strike | Training Range`** in the server list.

Only Blue has slots. The opposition (targets, SAMs, adversary fighters, the
hot zone's CAP) is AI. You can start from:

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Andersen AFB** and **Antonio B. Won Pat Intl** (Guam) | Dynamic spawn with hot start: pick any airframe |
| **CVN-72** (Supercarrier) | F/A-18C x4, F-14B x2, F-14A x2 |
| **LHA-1** (Tarawa) | AV-8B x2, UH-1H x2 |
| Combined Arms | Tactical commander, JTAC/operator and observer slots |

Scripts can't air-start players, so everyone launches from a runway or a deck,
the way a real exercise launches from Andersen.

When you spawn, the server greets you in the message box:

```dcsmessage
Welcome to the Vector Strike Range, Mariana Islands. The thin outlines on the F10 map are the real range complex airspace and the filled areas inside are the ranges; every one has a job and F10 > Range > Sectors lists them. The missile trainer is ON. Debriefs and spawning: range.vectorstrike.org
```

## How it differs from the campaign

| Campaign server | Training range |
| --- | --- |
| Lives, points, a side for the round | None of it. Every slot is open |
| Kills move the war | Kills and impacts are graded and posted |
| Missiles kill you | The **missile trainer** removes a missile that would have killed you and tells you so (you can turn it off) |
| The F10 menu runs the war | **F10 > Range** runs the drills, set-ups and spawns |

Guns and AAA are always real. The missile trainer can't stop a bullet. See
[Missile Trainer, Threats and Air Combat](./missile-defence.md).

## Fly it like the real thing

The briefing asks you to treat it as a real range:

- Launch from Andersen or Won Pat, or from the decks.
- Work inside your airspace. **R-7201** round Farallon de Medinilla is the only
  live impact area at sea.
- Tankers sit on the published tracks: fly the entry line from the ARIP, join
  at the ARCP, leave on the exit line.

## The range site

**https://range.vectorstrike.org** is the range's own site. It has:

- the live range picture: sectors, players, stations, tankers, carriers and threats
- every graded result as a card, with a debrief for each
- the greenie board and the leaderboards
- your own pilot record, with qualifications and coaching notes
- a spawner for adversaries, targets, tankers and ships
- a range brief you can print as a kneeboard card, and bombing, wind-over-deck
  and tanker calculators

You log in with the same Discord account you use on the Vector Strike
dashboard. To see your own record or use the spawner, your Discord must be
linked to your DCS pilot: `/linkme` in Discord gives you a code, then
`-linkme <code>` in DCS chat on any Vector Strike server. See
[Range Results and Debriefs](./results.md).
