# Info Menu

`F10 → Info` is the engine's reference desk. Everything on it is a **read-only
report, free, and available from any slot** — fighter, helo, or transport. If
you are ever unsure what is going on, this is the menu to open first.

```
F10 → Info
├── My Status              your points, lives, sorties, kills
├── Situation              the auto-generated briefing, 6 pages
│   ├── 1. Overview
│   ├── 2. Tasking
│   ├── 3. Hotspots
│   ├── 4. Threats & Air
│   ├── 5. Logistics
│   └── 6. Comms
├── Support & Radios       live AWACS / tankers / JTACs and their frequencies
├── Supply Convoys         friendly convoys in transit, and how far out they are
├── Navaids Directory      every friendly TACAN / NDB / ICLS on the map
├── Time & Server          mission time, restart schedule
├── Weather                the weather brief for your position
└── Help                   six in-cockpit help topics
```

## My Status

Your own record, as the engine has it:

- Your name and coalition.
- Point balance.
- Current kill streak and career kills.
- Whether sides are locked, and how many side switches you get this round.
- Lives remaining per life type — **only when the server is enforcing lives.**
  It is not on the live mission, so this block is normally absent. See
  [Points and Lives](../gameplay/points-and-lives.md).

Same information as the `-balance` / `-lives` chat commands, without leaving the
cockpit.

## Situation

The full six-page auto-generated briefing for your coalition. Nobody writes it —
the engine reads the campaign state and **your side's sensor picture** and ranks
what matters right now.

| Page | What's on it |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Overview | Headline, territory share, how the round is trending |
| 2. Tasking | Your coalition's ranked task list — including everything on the [tasking board](../gameplay/tasking-board.md) |
| 3. Hotspots | Where the fighting actually is |
| 4. Threats & Air | Known enemy SAMs and air activity |
| 5. Logistics | Supply state, what's starving, what's cut off |
| 6. Comms | Frequencies — GCI, ATC, flight channels |

It is a **coalition picture, not ground truth**: it only contains what your side
has actually detected. An enemy SAM nobody has seen is not on page 4. Read
[The Auto-Generated Briefing](../gameplay/briefing.md) for how the intel decays
and what earns you a better picture.

The same report reaches you in two other places: a short panel about 20 seconds
after slot entry, and the dashboard's Briefing tab. `-brief` in chat gives you
the short version any time.

## Support & Radios

Every friendly AWACS, tanker and JTAC currently alive, with the frequency and
TACAN to reach it. This is the page to check *before* you ask on comms whether
there's a tanker up.

If it says "No active AWACS, tankers, or JTACs" — somebody needs to spend points
in `Actions` and put one up. See [Calling AWACS, Tankers & CAP](../playbooks/calling-support.md).

## Supply Convoys

Friendly supply convoys in transit, where they are going, and how many metres
they still have to run. Useful two ways:

- **Defensively** — a convoy in transit is the thing keeping a front-line base
  in the fight. Know where yours are.
- **Offensively** — the enemy has the same page. Their convoys are a legitimate
  target worth points, and killing them starves the base at the far end. See
  [Logistics & Supply](../gameplay/logistics.md).

## Navaids Directory

Every navaid your side currently owns: TACAN channels, NDB homers, carrier
ICLS/ACLS/Link-4, with the Morse ident for each. These are **generated by the
engine per round** and follow ownership — capture a FARP and it re-lights on
your side's channel pool.

Full explanation in [Navaids & Approaches](../gameplay/navaids.md).

## Time & Server

Mission time and whether a restart is scheduled. If it says "Server restart: not
scheduled automatically", the round ends on the campaign's own reset condition
(on the live mission: one side holding 95% of the map), not on a clock.

## Weather

The weather brief for where you are — the same data the auto-briefing puts on
page 1. On the live server this is pulled from real-world METAR, so it changes
between rounds and is worth checking before a low-level or a carrier recovery.

Also available as `-weather` in chat.

## Help

Six short in-cockpit topics, for when you cannot alt-tab:

- Getting Started
- Chat Commands
- Cargo & Logistics
- Objectives & Capturing
- Carrier Groups
- Combat & JTAC

They are deliberately terse. This wiki is the long version.
