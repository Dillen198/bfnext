# Your Task & Rookie Mode

Every time you take a slot, the engine hands you **one task**: the most urgent
job on your side's ranked tasking list that your aircraft can actually do. It
arrives with your slot briefing, and a zone is drawn on **your own** F10 map.
Nobody else sees your zone.

```
YOUR TASK: <task> -- x1.5 points and bonus XP inside the zone marked on your map
```

You don't have to take it. But the tasking list is built from what the war
actually needs: bases under attack, cut supply lines, enemy air defence in the
way. Flying your task is the quickest way to help.

## What the zone pays

| Inside your task zone | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| A kill you make yourself | **1.5×** the normal points, plus 150 bonus XP |
| A capture, repair, supply run or FARP at an objective in the zone | 300 bonus XP |

The zone has a 15 km radius for most tasks, 25 km for recon and 40 km for
intercepts. It lasts two hours, or until you leave the slot.

Kills credited to you through AI (artillery, an AI flight you called) pay
the normal amount. Troop drops and deployed units earn their usual XP, but no
zone bonus.

Cargo aircraft get ground jobs (logistics, CSAR and captures), and everything
else gets combat and recon tasks. The task arrives with the slot briefing, so
a server that has the slot briefing turned off hands out no tasks.

## Rookie mode

For your first **5 sorties** on the server, your task is picked from the
safest useful work: logistics, recon, CSAR or holding a friendly base, rather
than a deep strike into the enemy's air defence. Your briefing also points you to
**F10 → Info → Help → Getting Started**, and to the First Sortie guide and the
training range on the dashboard.

A sortie counts from your first takeoff in a slot. Once you've flown 5, you
are a veteran for good: that carries over to every later round, so you only
get rookie mode once. A count short of 5 starts again with a new round.
Pilots who were already on the server before rookie mode arrived count as
veterans.
