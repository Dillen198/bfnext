# Your Task & Rookie Mode

Every time you take an aircraft slot, the engine hands you **one task**: the
most urgent job on your side's ranked tasking list that your aircraft can
actually do. It arrives with your slot briefing, and a zone is drawn on **your
own** F10 map. Nobody else sees your zone.

```dcsmessage
YOUR TASK: RESUPPLY Gori -- x1.5 points and bonus XP inside the zone marked on your map (15 km)
```

The map mark at the centre of the zone reads
`YOUR TASK: RESUPPLY Gori -- x1.5 points and bonus XP within 15 km`.

You don't have to take it. But the tasking list is built from what the war
actually needs: bases under attack, cut supply lines, enemy air defence in the
way. Flying your task is the quickest way to help.

## What the zone pays

| Inside your task zone | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| A kill you make yourself | **1.5×** the normal points, plus 150 bonus XP |
| A capture, repair, supply run or FARP at an objective in the zone | 300 bonus XP |

The zone has a 15 km radius for most tasks, 25 km for recon and 40 km for
intercepts. It lasts two hours, or until you leave the slot. A new slot gives
you a new task and replaces the old zone.

Kills credited to you through AI (your deployed SAMs and troops, an AI flight
you called) pay the normal amount. Troop drops and deployed units earn their
usual XP, but no zone bonus. The 1.5× is applied before underdog pay and the
high-balance rate (see [Points and Lives](./points-and-lives.md)).

Cargo aircraft (anything that can carry cargo) get ground jobs: logistics,
CSAR and captures. Everything else gets combat, defence and recon tasks. The
task arrives with the slot briefing, so a server that has the slot briefing
turned off hands out no tasks, and you get none when your side's list has
nothing your aircraft can do.

## Rookie mode

For your first **5 sorties** on the server, your task is picked from the
safest useful work: logistics, recon, CSAR or holding a friendly base, rather
than a deep strike into the enemy's air defence. Your briefing adds:

```dcsmessage
NEW PILOT: this is a safer task to start with. F10 > Info > Help > Getting Started covers the basics, and the First Sortie guide and the training range are on the dashboard.
```

A sortie counts from your first takeoff in a slot; landing to rearm and going
again is the same sortie. Once you've flown 5, you are a veteran for good:
that carries over to every later round, so you only get rookie mode once. A
count short of 5 starts again with a new round. Pilots who were already on the
server before rookie mode arrived count as veterans.
