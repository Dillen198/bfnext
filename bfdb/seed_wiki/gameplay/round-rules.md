# Rear Bases, Era Weapons & Round Length

Each server sets these rules for itself, and each one only applies where the
server's config turns it on. Check the server's comms and rules card, or ask
staff.

## Protected rear bases

Each side can have a few protected rear airfields, so nobody spawns into a
fight they never chose. While its own side still holds the base, an **enemy
aircraft within 15 nm** of it gets **60 seconds in total** in that airspace.
After that it is destroyed and the pilot goes to spectators.

```
PROTECTED AIRSPACE: <base> is a rear base. Leave now -- 45s before you are removed.
```

You are warned every 10 seconds. The 60 seconds are a running total for the
sortie, not a fresh allowance each visit; it only resets when you leave the
slot. Every removal is logged for staff. A server can exempt aircraft flying below a set
height above the ground, such as a helicopter crossing at treetop level.

## Era weapons

A Cold War or early-era round can ban weapons from that era's list, for
example AMRAAMs in an 80s round.

- **If you take off carrying one,** you get a warning and **5 minutes to land**.
- **If you fire one, or are still flying when time is up,** your aircraft is
  destroyed and you go to spectators.

Check your loadout before you taxi: the rearm screen will still let you load a
banned weapon.

## Round length

A server can cap how long a round runs. If nobody has won when the cap is
reached, the round ends in a **draw** and a new one starts. This lets a
theatre rotation move on from a front that has frozen.
