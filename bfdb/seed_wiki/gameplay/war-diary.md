# The War Diary

Every day of the campaign gets its own front page. **WAR DIARY** in the
dashboard sidebar (next to SITREP, TACMAP and OBJECTIVES) shows it, and you
don't need to sign in to read it.

It's written from what actually happened that day on the server: the bases
that changed hands, where the fighting was, what each side lost, and who flew
best. It's laid out like an old broadsheet newspaper.

## What's on the page

| Section | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Masthead and headlines | The date, the campaign day, and the day's main story. The masthead says **"Complete returns to the close of the day"** once the day is filed, or **"Wire reports still arriving"** while it's still running |
| **Official Returns** | The state of the war tonight |
| **Changed Hands** | Every base captured that day, in order. "None. The line held." on a quiet day |
| **Where the Fighting Was** | The fighting, grouped by area of the front |
| **In the Air** | The air war that day |
| **Ace of the Day** | The day's best pilot, with a picture of the aircraft they flew most |
| **The Ledger** | Equipment destroyed, **To-day** and **Since the Opening**, and the ground each country holds |
| **Previous Editions** | Earlier days, with thumbnails |

To link to a past day, add `?ed=YYYY-MM-DD` to the page address.

## The pictures

Each day has an illustration of that day's real story (a town being taken, a
burnt-out convoy, a SAM site after a strike) with period-correct equipment for
the campaign, printed as a halftone like an old newspaper photo.

The running day gets an **early-edition** picture about three hours into the
UTC day, captioned "Early-edition illustration". When the day is filed, the
picture is redrawn to match the full story.

## Both sides read the same paper

The War Diary is **not** fog-of-war limited. Blue and Red read the same
report, the way both sides of a real war read the same newspapers. It only
covers what has already happened, never where anyone is now.

Where the server has it set up, each new day's edition is also posted to
Discord.
