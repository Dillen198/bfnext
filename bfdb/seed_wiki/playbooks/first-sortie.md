# Your First Sortie

You've connected, you're staring at the slot list, and it's clearly *not* "join
and fly." This page is the 20-minute version: what to do, in order, your first
time on the server. Every step links to the page with the full detail.

## Before you spawn

1. **Read the briefing.** The in-game briefing (and the
   [Live Ops dashboard](./reading-live-ops.md)) tells you which coalition is on
   the back foot, where the front line is, and what the current objective is.
2. **Pick a side and a slot.** Choose **BLUFOR** or **REDFOR** and take any
   aircraft slot for that side. The engine registers you to that coalition the
   first moment you take a slot — you don't have to type anything. (You *can*
   still type `blue` or `red` in chat from spectator if you prefer.) Your
   coalition is **permanent** unless the server grants side switches — see
   [Joining a Team](../getting-started/joining-team.md).
3. **Know your role's life pool.** Lives are tracked per role (Standard,
   Attack, Intercept, Logistics, Recon). Type `-lives` to see your count. A
   logistics helo has 6 lives; a fast jet has 3. See
   [Points and Lives](../gameplay/points-and-lives.md).

## Your first flight — pick one

You don't need to win the war on sortie one. Pick the lane that matches your
slot and do one useful thing.

### If you're in a transport helo (Mi-8, Huey, CH-47, Hip)

**Run a crate or a troop lift.** This is the highest-impact thing a new player
can do and it's almost impossible to mess up badly.

- Land at a friendly airbase/FARP, `F10 → Cargo → Load` a crate (or
  `F10 → Troops → Load` an infantry squad).
- Fly it to a forward friendly objective that's low on supply and
  `F10 → Cargo → Unload`.
- Full walkthrough: [Running Crates & Building a Base](./running-crates.md).

### If you're in a strike/CAS jet (A-10, JF-17, F-16, Su-25, etc.)

**Work a JTAC target.** A JTAC is a ground/drone spotter that finds targets and
lasers them for you.

- `F10 → JTAC → [pick a JTAC] → Status` — it tells you what it's looking at and
  its **laser code**.
- Set your weapon's laser code to match, fly to the area, drop on the lased
  target.
- Full walkthrough: [Flying CAS With a JTAC](./cas-with-jtac.md).

### If you're in a fighter (F-15, F/A-18, MiG-29, Su-27)

**Fly a CAP for your side.** Check the [EWR picture](../f10-menu/ewr.md)
(`F10 → EWR → Report`), push toward the front, and protect the transport and
CAS players who are actually moving the front line. Air kills are **25 points**
each — that's your deployment fund for later.

### If you're brand new to DCS multiplayer

Take a **Recon** slot (L-39, TF-51, Yak-52 — 6 lives). Fly near an enemy
objective and you'll reveal its units on the F10 map for your whole coalition —
see [Reconnaissance](../f10-menu/recon.md). Low risk, genuinely useful, and you
learn the map.

## After you land

- **RTB to a friendly base and land** to bank the sortie and keep your life.
- Check `-balance` — you earn points for kills, captures, transport, and recon.
- Points buy deployables and support missions:
  [Calling AWACS, Tankers & CAP](./calling-support.md).

## The one rule that trips up everyone

You **cannot load troops/crates from, or unload them at, an objective that is
threatened or itself capturable.** If the game says *"you can't deploy troops
here while enemies are near,"* the objective has enemies within range — wait
~5 minutes after they leave, or stage from a base further back.
