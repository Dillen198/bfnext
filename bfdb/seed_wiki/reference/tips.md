# Tips & Best Practices

Hard-earned lessons from pilots who already learned them the expensive way.

## Before you take off

1. **Read the briefing.** `-brief`, or `F10 → Info → Situation`. It is generated
   from your coalition's actual sensor picture and it ranks what matters right
   now. Two minutes there saves a wasted 40-minute sortie.

2. **Check whether support is already up.** `F10 → Info → Support & Radios`
   lists every live AWACS, tanker and JTAC with its frequency. Don't spend 100
   points on a second AWACS.

3. **Write down the navaid.** `F10 → Info → Navaids Directory`. FARPs, hubs and
   carriers get channels generated fresh each round, and a cockpit menu is a bad
   place to be reading when you are low on fuel in weather.

4. **Know the weather.** `-weather`. On this server it comes from real-world
   METAR, so it changes between rounds.

## Fighting

5. **EWR is delayed.** The picture is roughly a minute old — about 8 nm of error
   on a fast mover. Treat a contact as a cue to look, not a firing solution.

6. **Beam and notch works here.** Detection probability is modelled by aspect
   and closure. Going beam with low closure genuinely makes you hard to see.

7. **Coordinate with a JTAC for anything that matters.** A laser on a confirmed
   target beats carpet-bombing a grid square, and a JTAC's eyes-on feeds the
   coalition intel map for everyone else too.

8. **A drone JTAC is 25 points.** It is the cheapest sensor in the game and it
   can lase. Buy one before you buy anything else.

## Taking ground

9. **Ask the Capture Advisor first.** `F10 → Objectives → Capture Advisor:
   Nearest` tells you exactly which precondition is failing. Most failed
   captures are a missing precondition, not a lack of firepower.

10. **Check your troop type can actually capture.** The advisor flags the ones
    that can't. Flying the wrong squad 60 nm is the most common wasted sortie on
    the server.

11. **A base that stops repairing has run dry.** That is your window — take it
    before a convoy gets through.

12. **The fight isn't over when the timer completes.** Consolidation follows,
    your troops have to stay in the zone, and the enemy can take it straight back
    by killing them. Delivering supply or a repair kit during the hold pushes
    the clock forward.

13. **Post a CAPTURE or SUPPLY task.** They close themselves out when the
    coalition finishes the job, so they cost you nothing to leave up.

## Logistics

14. **Logistics pays like combat.** A repair kit or a supply transfer delivered
    is worth about what an air kill is. The run nobody wants to fly is not
    charity.

15. **Guard your convoys, hunt theirs.** Automated convoys are soft targets and
    the enemy knows it. `F10 → Info → Supply Convoys` shows yours in transit.

16. **Cut the road, not the base.** Supply routing follows the front line. Take
    the objectives astride an enemy supply route and everything behind it
    starves without you ever attacking it.

17. **Factories and logistics hubs are the real targets.** A factory is worth
    four airbases to enemy war production, a hub three — and damage counts, not
    just ownership.

18. **Order an AI helo when nobody will fly the run.** You can dispatch troop
    insertion or resupply from a fast jet. Escort it if the route is hot.

## Housekeeping

19. **Look for `More >>`.** DCS drops anything past the tenth menu entry; the
    engine pages the rest into `More >>`. If something is missing, look there.

20. **Re-open `Actions>>` after placing a map mark.** The menu captures your
    marks when it is built.

21. **Delete stale map marks and stale tasks.** Twelve task slots per coalition,
    shared. A board full of hour-old CAS pins is worse than an empty one.

22. **Talk before a big push.** Three coordinated pilots beat six lone wolves,
    every time. The tasking board and the GCI net both exist to make that
    cheaper than a Discord call.
