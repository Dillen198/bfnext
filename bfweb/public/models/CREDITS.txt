3D models used by the dashboard's flight replay (bfweb/src/replay/theater.ts),
the dashboard's wireframe art (public/media/wire, from scripts/gen-wireframes.py)
and the range site's 3D debriefs.

Every *.glb in this folder is based on the 3D meshes of Tacview by Raia
Software Inc. (https://www.tacview.net), published at
https://github.com/Vyrtuoz/Tacview ("3D Models/Data/Meshes") under the GNU
General Public License version 3. Each file keeps the name of the Tacview
mesh it comes from (FixedWing.F-16.glb is FixedWing.F-16.obj).

Changes: converted from OBJ to GLB by bfweb/scripts/build-tacview-models.mjs
(re-oriented nose +Y / up +Z, recentred, positions only). These files are
themselves licensed under the GPL-3.0: see LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt in this folder.
The source meshes are at the address above, and the conversion script is in
this repository.

Which DCS unit type is drawn with which mesh, and the names and engagement
ranges the replay shows, come from Tacview's object database
(https://github.com/Vyrtuoz/Tacview, "Database/Default Properties", GPL-3.0),
made into shared/models/tacview.json by bfweb/scripts/gen-tacview-db.py.
